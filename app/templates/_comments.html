Two consequences worth knowing. The open window has its own URL, so it can be linked and it survives a reload. And a notification link of the form /i/12#c34 opens the window straight onto that comment, through `.overlay:has(:target)` -- which is why a reply from your inbox lands you in the conversation rather than at the top of the page. Everything inside is still a plain form post, so voting and commenting work with scripting switched off entirely. #}
